WHO WE ARE

About Mogale Tennis Association

🎾

Building a stronger tennis community

Mogale Tennis Association is committed to promoting and developing tennis within the community.

Our goal is to create opportunities for players of different ages and abilities to participate in tennis, improve their skills and compete in a positive sporting environment.

Through coaching, tournaments, competitions and community activities, we aim to develop talented players while encouraging discipline, teamwork and a healthy lifestyle.
