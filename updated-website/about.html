ABOUT US

More Than Just Tennis

Mogale Tennis Association is a community-focused tennis organisation based in Mogale City, Gauteng, South Africa.

The association aims to promote tennis and create opportunities for players of different ages and skill levels. We believe that sport can play an important role in developing confidence, discipline, teamwork and healthy lifestyles.

Through training programmes, tournaments, junior development activities and community events, we provide an environment where players can learn, compete and grow.

Our vision is to contribute towards a stronger tennis culture within the Mogale City community while creating opportunities for future players.
